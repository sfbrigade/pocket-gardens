# Pocket Gardens

Pocket Gardens records individual growing areas and the plants and people connected to them.

## Language

**Plot**:
An individually identifiable growing area, such as one tree well, bed, or continuous strip. Several plots can share an address.
_Avoid_: Property or address as a synonym for plot.

**Garden**:
The user-facing name for a plot, rather than a separate entity or collection of plots.
_Avoid_: Garden as a separate level above plot.

**Garden stage**:
The lifecycle condition of a plot, such as potential, preparing, or active. It describes the garden's actual condition independently of whether permission has been confirmed.
_Avoid_: Permission or review status as a synonym for garden stage.

**Permission status**:
What is known about permission to garden at a particular plot. An active garden can have unknown or unconfirmed permission.
_Avoid_: Garden stage as a synonym for permission status.

**Plant directory**:
The catalogue of named plants available to select when recording what is planned or growing in a plot.
_Avoid_: A plot's planting inventory or planting history as a synonym for the directory.

**Plot planting**:
A plant selected from the plant directory and recorded once as planned or currently growing in a particular plot, with an optional quantity and notes.
_Avoid_: An unlinked typed plant name as a completed plot planting.

**Planting history**:
The record of individual planting events at a plot. It is distinct from the list of plants currently growing there or planned for it.
_Avoid_: Current planting inventory as a synonym for planting history.

**Carer**:
A platform user who looks after a plot.
_Avoid_: A submitter or an unverified typed name as a synonym for an assigned carer.

**Care offer**:
A community member's expression of willingness to look after a plot. An offer is distinct from an administrator's carer assignment.
_Avoid_: Confirmed assignment as a synonym for care offer.

**Volunteer record**:
An existing record of a person in the gardening community, which can include care assignments and activity history even when that person has no platform account.
_Avoid_: Platform user as a synonym for an unlinked volunteer record.

**Platform user**:
A person with an account used to sign in to Pocket Gardens. A user can be linked to their existing volunteer record.
_Avoid_: An imported contact detail as a synonym for a platform account.
